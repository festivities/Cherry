### Text moderation (stacked-mark crash guard, banned words; 2026-11-27)

- **The crash.** A text with a long run of combining marks (Unicode Mn/Me) stacked on one character crashes the stock client of the players who draw it. Seen in production on 2026-11-22..27: copy-pasted Thai strings (a consonant, or the dotted circle U+25CC, carrying 20 to 100 tone and vowel marks) sent to Squares, a few to whispers, from about 30 accounts; the heaviest sender repeated one 49-mark string every 25 to 45 s on 2026-11-27 evening, and players reported being kicked out each time. Evidence (method: `chat_log` run lengths against the journal's gateway `OBS :10000 read … connection reset by peer` lines; the journal keeps only about 5 h, so only messages after 15:45 that day could be correlated): runs of 49+ were followed within 3 s by 6.2 resets on average against 1.1 in the 9 s before (n = 27, 70 % with 5 or more); runs of 47 in a busy Square by 8 to 10; runs of 3 to 4 by 0.1 (n = 38); controls 0.7. Runs of 5 to 46 mostly showed none, so the client's threshold lies somewhere in the 30s or 40s [I]. The client-side cause (layout, texture size) was not traced; the server rule does not depend on it.
- **Legitimate text.** Of 1.81 M chat lines: runs of 1 to 2 in 268 k (a Thai vowel plus a tone mark), 3 in 262 and 4 in 88 (Thai emphasis: a tone mark or vowel typed three or four times), 5 to 8 rare (longer emphasis; kaomoji such as a crying face with five marks under each eye, run 6), 9 to 20 in about 20 lines, nearly all copies of the attack strings. Every stored text (nicknames, Status Messages, Diary, guestbook, 1:1 history, Circle) had runs of 5 or less on 2026-11-27, and every created avatar's nickname had 20 runes or fewer and was not blank (one held a newline).
- **The rule** (`internal/httpx/text.go`: `SafeText`, `MaxMarks = 8`): valid UTF-8 and no run of more than 8 marks. A format character (Cf: zero-width joiner, word joiner, tag characters) neither counts nor ends a run, so a run cannot be split invisibly (renderers skip those when they stack marks) while emoji ZWJ sequences and tag flags still pass; any other character ends the run. Enforced at three places, which together take every text a player sends:
  - every JSON request body: `httpx.UnmarshalNativeJSON` runs `SafeJSON` over all keys and values with their `\u` escapes decoded (ASCII-only bodies skip the scan) and returns `ErrUnsafeText`, which each route answers with its usual refusal (400 or its error body).
